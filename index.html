Anticoagulation Recommendations for PE

AHA/ACC/ACCP/ACEP/CHEST/SCAI/SHM/SIR/SVM/SVN Guideline for Evaluation and Management of Pulmonary Embolism (2026)

General Recommendation: In patients with acute PE who do not have any contraindications to anticoagulation therapy, anticoagulation therapy should be initiated to reduce risk of recurrent VTE and death. COR 1 LOE B-R

AHA/ACC Category Preferred Agent Rationale COR LOE
A1–C1 Oral: DOAC
(Apixaban, Rivaroxaban, Edoxaban, Dabigatran)		 DOACs preferred over VKAs. Comparable efficacy with lower risk of major bleeding (especially ICH). 1 B-R
A1–C1 Parenteral: Enoxaparin 1 mg/kg SQ q12h LMWH preferred over UFH. Predictable pharmacokinetics; no routine monitoring required. 1 B-R

Contraindications to Fibrinolytic Therapy

Adapted from AHA/ACC Guidelines. Viewed as advisory for clinical decision-making and may not be all-inclusive or definitive.

Absolute Contraindications
Any prior intracranial hemorrhage (ICH)
Known structural cerebral vascular lesion (e.g., AVM)
Known malignant intracranial neoplasm (primary or metastatic)
Ischemic stroke within 3 months (except acute ischemic stroke <4.5h onset*)
Suspected aortic dissection
Active bleeding or bleeding diathesis (excluding menses)
Significant closed-head or facial trauma within 3 months
Intracranial or intraspinal surgery within 2 months
Severe uncontrolled hypertension unresponsive to therapy (SBP >180 mmHg or DBP >110 mmHg)
Relative Contraindications
History of chronic, severe, poorly controlled hypertension
Significant hypertension on presentation (SBP >180 mmHg or DBP >110 mmHg)
History of prior ischemic stroke >3 months
Dementia
Known intracranial pathology not covered in absolute contraindications
Traumatic or prolonged (>10 min) CPR
Major surgery within 3 weeks
Recent (within 2–4 weeks) internal bleeding
Noncompressible vascular punctures
Pregnancy
Active peptic ulcer
Oral anticoagulant therapy

* Acute ischemic stroke within 4.5 hours of onset.
CPR: cardiopulmonary resuscitation • DBP: diastolic blood pressure • ICH: intracranial hemorrhage • SBP: systolic blood pressure

Systemic Thrombolytics for Pulmonary Embolism

Category E / Massive / High-Risk PE

Standard Dose

Alteplase 100 mg IV over 2 hours

Reduced Dose

Alteplase 50 mg IV over 2 hours

Consider with elevated bleeding risk: weight <60 kg, age >75, recent surgery, or recent non-intracranial bleeding

Category E2 / Cardiac Arrest Due to Massive PE

Cardiac Arrest Dosing

Alteplase 50 mg IV over 2 minutes

Followed by at least 15 minutes of CPR. May repeat if ROSC not achieved.
